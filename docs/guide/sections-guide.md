# Managing Career Sections

MySirati structures your professional achievements into five dedicated sections, each supporting atomic reordering and item-level resource links.

---

## 1. Work Experiences

Document your employment history, contract roles, and internships:
- **Company Name**: The organization or client name.
- **Role / Job Title**: Your formal position.
- **Dates**: Start date and end date (or mark as "Current Position").
- **Location**: On-site city, hybrid, or "Remote".
- **Summary & Highlights**: Bulleted or paragraph descriptions of accomplishments, architectural decisions, and metrics.
- **Resource Links**: Links to deployed products, press releases, or company websites.

## 2. Education & Certifications

Record academic degrees and professional certifications:
- **Institution**: University, college, or bootcamp name.
- **Degree / Study Type**: Bachelor's, Master's, Certificate, or Course.
- **Field of Study**: Major or specialization (e.g. Computer Science).
- **Dates**: Start date and completion date.
- **Certificate Link**: URL to credential verification or official diploma.

## 3. Projects Showcase

Highlight your open-source projects, side endeavors, or research contributions:
- **Project Name**: Title of the project.
- **Summary**: Concise description of what problem the project solves.
- **Tech Stack / Keywords**: Comma-separated technologies used (e.g. React, PostgreSQL, Docker).
- **Resource Links**: GitHub repository, live web demo, or whitepaper.

## 4. Skills Matrix

Group and categorize your technical and soft skills:
- **Skill Name**: E.g. "TypeScript", "PostgreSQL", "System Architecture".
- **Category**: E.g. "Languages", "Databases", "Cloud / DevOps", "Frameworks".
- **Proficiency Level**: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.

## 5. Languages

List spoken and written natural languages:
- **Language**: E.g. "English", "French", "Arabic", "Spanish".
- **Fluency Level**: Native, Fluent, Professional Working, Limited, or Elementary.

---

## Section Reordering

All sections support instant position ordering:
- Use the **Up** / **Down** arrow buttons on any item to swap positions.
- Changes are saved immediately and atomically to the database.
- Order is strictly preserved when exported to JSON Resume.
